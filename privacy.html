privacy

-------

This site collects nothing. There is no analytics script, no advertising network, no embedded tracker, no newsletter signup, and no contact form. No cookies are set.

One thing is stored locally in your browser: your light or dark preference, under the key rl-theme. It never leaves your device and I cannot read it. Clearing site data removes it.

The host that serves these files keeps standard server logs, which include IP addresses and user agents. I do not review them and I do not connect them to anyone. Links out to GitHub and other sites are governed by those sites' own policies.

If you email me at ryanlu@ucsb.edu, I keep the email if I want to. That is the only personal data I hold, and I will delete it on request.
