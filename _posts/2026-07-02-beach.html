The best window at any beach in Los Angeles is between about five thirty and seven thirty in the morning. The parking is free because nobody is collecting yet, the water is glass before the onshore wind picks up, and the whole place belongs to maybe nine people who all nod at each other.

By ten it is a different location entirely. Same sand, same water, but the wind has come up, the lot is full, and the ocean has turned into texture. People who only go to the beach at noon on a Saturday have a completely reasonable and completely wrong opinion about what beaches are like.

Nothing about the morning version requires planning, which is the point. No ticket, no reservation, no group text. You set an alarm, you put a towel in the car the night before, and you are back home before most people are awake.
