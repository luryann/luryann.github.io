--- layout: post navKey: writing published: false title: "an album is a different object when you are high" description: "What changes about listening, and what to do with it the next day." ---

Something happens to attention. Sober, I listen to a song the way I read a paragraph, front to back, mostly for what it says. High, the whole thing widens out and I stop hearing a song at all. I hear a room with instruments in it, each one somewhere specific, and the singer standing a foot closer than I remembered.

The details that surface are almost always things that were already there. The bass moving under a chorus I have heard fifty times. A breath before a line. A room reverb on one snare hit and not the next. It is not that the record changed, it is that the parts I normally filter out stopped getting filtered.

It also ruins your judgment about whether something is good, and I have learned not to trust anything I decide in that state. The useful part is the next day, going back sober to check what I actually found. Some of it is real and stays. The rest was just me being amazed that music exists, which is fine too.
