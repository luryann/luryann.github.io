The trips I remember are not the long ones. Friday afternoon to Sunday evening, two hours up the coast, one bag, no plan past where to sleep. Short enough that nothing needs arranging, cheap enough that a bad forecast does not ruin an investment.

Los Angeles is unreasonably good for this. Desert in one direction, mountains in another, a whole coastline in the third, all inside a tank of gas. Most of what stops people is the belief that going somewhere requires a week off work.

My whole kit is a thirty liter pack and it takes ten minutes to fill because the list never changes. Lowering the cost of leaving is what makes you actually go.
